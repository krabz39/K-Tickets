Withdrawal control

A withdrawal request is a financial claim against your merchant account. K-Tickets keeps the request pending until the payout is reviewed and processed.

Ownership Requests are created against your authenticated merchant ID.
Review Merchants cannot approve or mark their own withdrawals as paid.
Audit trail Processing status, reviewer and payout references are retained by the withdrawal record.
